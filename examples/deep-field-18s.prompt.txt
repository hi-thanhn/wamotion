Make an 18-second film, 1920×1080, from three real James Webb Space Telescope images in data/: Webb's First Deep Field (galaxy cluster SMACS 0723), the Cosmic Cliffs of the Carina Nebula, and the Pillars of Creation. data/SOURCE.md gives each file, what it shows, the distances and sizes you may quote, the licence (CC BY 4.0) and the exact credit line that must appear on screen at the end. A 3D journey through these real pictures: separate each image into depth layers (foreground stars, mid structure, the far background) and move a camera through them with real parallax, so the viewer travels into the photograph rather than panning across it; pass from one object to the next without a hard cut if you can. Text is only the real names, distances in light-years and the credit, in English, small and exact. Mood: planetarium, slow, very large, silent in the way space is silent until something enormous arrives. Purpose: make a developer feel the scale with nothing invented. Sound is half the film: space has no sound, so what is heard is the instrument and the room, deep and slow, with one swell for the arrival at each object; mix it as one 18-second piece. Build it with Remotion in this folder and render to out/deep-field.mp4.
