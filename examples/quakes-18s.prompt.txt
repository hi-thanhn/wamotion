Make an 18-second film, 1920×1080, from thirty days of real earthquakes: data/usgs-4.5-month.geojson, 665 events of magnitude 4.5 and up, 2026-10-10 to 2026-11-09, the U.S. Geological Survey feed (public domain; data/SOURCE.md gives the URL, the fields and the credit line). The Earth at night, as a 3D globe or a map, your choice: the thirty days are compressed into the film so every quake strikes in true order, at its true place, with magnitude as size and as sound, depth as colour or pitch if you want it. On-screen text is only what the data says: dates, magnitudes, place names, a running count, and the credit "Earthquake data: U.S. Geological Survey" near the end. All text in English.

Mood: a night observatory, quiet and exact; the large ones should be felt in the room. Purpose: a data film that makes a viewer sit still for the magnitude 7.0. Sound is half the film: every quake is heard as well as seen, scaled to its magnitude, and the whole eighteen seconds is mixed as one piece with a shape, not 665 identical blips. Build it with Remotion in this folder and render to out/quakes.mp4.
