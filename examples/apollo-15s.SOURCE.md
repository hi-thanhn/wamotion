# Data source

`apollo11-eagle-has-landed-extended.mp3` (14.8 s, 44.1 kHz) and `apollo11-eagle-has-landed.mp3` (5.4 s, 16 kHz mono)
are NASA's own releases of the Apollo 11 air-to-ground loop at the moment of landing, 20 July 1969, 20:17 UTC
(mission elapsed time about 102:45:40 to 102:46:17). Downloaded 2026-11-09 from NASA's "Historical Sounds" page:

- https://www.nasa.gov/historical-sounds/
- https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/590333main_ringtone_eagleHasLanded_extended.mp3
- https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/569462main_eagle_has_landed.mp3
- `apollo11-one-small-step.mp3` (13.0 s) from the same page, https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/590331main_ringtone_smallStep.mp3, kept as a spare.

Usage: NASA states its audio, video and images "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States" and asks
that "NASA should be acknowledged as the source of the material" (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
The page itself says the sounds may be downloaded and mixed. Credit line: "Audio: NASA".

## Official transcript of this exchange

From the Apollo 11 Technical Air-to-Ground Voice Transcription (NASA, 1969, public domain), GET 102:45:40–102:46:17.
Speakers: Neil Armstrong (CDR, in the Lunar Module Eagle on the Moon) and Charles Duke (CAPCOM, Mission Control, Houston).
The extended clip starts at Armstrong's call; check the audio yourself for the exact word times, this list gives the words
and their order only. The short Quindar beeps are the real keying tones of the ground transmitter.

    102:45:40  Aldrin (LMP):  Contact light.                      (may be cut from the clip)
    102:45:43  Armstrong:     Shutdown.                           (may be cut from the clip)
    102:45:44  Aldrin:        Okay. Engine stop.                  (may be cut from the clip)
    102:45:58  Armstrong:     Houston, Tranquility Base here. The Eagle has landed.
    102:46:06  Duke (CAPCOM): Roger, Twan— Tranquility, we copy you on the ground.
                              You got a bunch of guys about to turn blue. We're breathing again. Thanks a lot.
    102:46:16  Aldrin:        Thank you.                          (may be cut from the clip)

Only the words that are actually audible in the clip should appear on screen.
