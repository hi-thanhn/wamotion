Make an 18-second film, 1920×1080, of the Earth at night as a real 3D globe: data/8k_earth_nightmap.jpg is a real night-lights texture of the planet (city lights from NASA's Earth at Night imagery; data/SOURCE.md gives the source, the licence and the credit line that must appear at the end), and data/cities-top20.json holds the twenty most populous cities with their coordinates and populations (Wikidata, CC0). The globe turns slowly through one revolution with the dark side toward the camera, the real city lights doing the work; as the terminator comes round, the sun rises over the limb once, and that is the one big moment. As the camera passes over them, a few of the twenty cities may be named with their population, in English, small, only real values from the data. Mood: the view from a space station, deep quiet, a slow breath. Purpose: a planet film that is beautiful because it is true. Sound is half the film: a deep bed from the start, the subtle presence of each city as it passes, and one accent when the sun breaks the limb; mix it as one 18-second piece with a shape. Build it with Remotion in this folder (a three.js scene inside Remotion is fine; it needs the right renderer flag) and render to out/night-earth.mp4.
