# Data source

Three James Webb Space Telescope images from ESA/Webb (esawebb.org), publication JPEG size, downloaded 2026-11-09.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://esawebb.org/copyright/ ; the film shows
the credit lines on screen and states that each image was separated into depth layers.

| Object | ESA/Webb id | Size | Page | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pillars of Creation, M16 (NIRCam) | weic2216b | 2309×4000 | https://esawebb.org/images/weic2216b/ | NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI; J. DePasquale, A. Pagan, A. M. Koekemoer (STScI) |
| Cosmic Cliffs, Carina Nebula NGC 3324 (NIRCam) | weic2205a | 4000×2317 | https://esawebb.org/images/weic2205a/ | NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI |
| Webb's First Deep Field, SMACS 0723 (NIRCam) | weic2209a | 3920×4000 | https://esawebb.org/images/weic2209a/ | NASA, ESA, CSA, and STScI |

Distances quoted on screen, from the release pages: Pillars about 6,500 light-years; Cosmic Cliffs about 7,600
light-years; SMACS 0723 about 4.6 billion light-years.

Note: the data folder handed to the agent contained weic2207a.jpg labelled as the Deep Field; that file is in fact
the Southern Ring Nebula. The agent read the embedded caption, noticed the mismatch, downloaded weic2209a itself and
recorded the swap. The two recorded sounds in the mix are CC0 clips from the bundled bank; the instrument is the
agent's own synthesis.
